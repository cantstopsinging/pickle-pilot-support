Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Pickle Pilot is designed to work locally on your device. Pickle Pilot does not require an account and does not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter into Pickle Pilot—such as player names, optional DUPR ratings, tournament details, schedules, scores, and standings—is stored locally on your device for the purpose of operating the app.

Data collection

Pickle Pilot itself does not collect or transmit the tournament and player information you enter to the developer’s servers.

Third-party services

Pickle Pilot does not currently require a developer-operated cloud account, advertising service, or analytics account in order to use its core features. Your device, operating system, app store, or distribution platform may process technical information under their own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

Pickle Pilot does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated server or account system.

Changes to this policy

If Pickle Pilot adds features in the future that change how information is handled, this policy may be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to cantstopsinging@gmail.com.
